# Design

The app has to feel like the Hartbeat website: calm, studio-made, nothing that looks generated.

## Brand

- **Name and wordmark:** Hartbeat, with an upside-down blue heart in place of the "a" (`components/Wordmark.tsx`). The heart beats at a calm 62 beats a minute.
- **Theme:** space and the northern lights, meaning limitlessness. Dark night sky behind, bright cards in front, lit by aurora glow so they pop forward.
- **Type:** Calibri Light for text and headings (Carlito is the free, metric-matched fallback that loads for everyone). Every number is in Helvetica.
- **Colour:** brand blue `#3280FD`. Health colours: Thriving green `#1EA97C`, Steady blue `#3280FD`, At risk amber `#F0A020`, Struggling red `#E5484D`. Status is always written in words too, never colour alone.

## Rules

- One sentence per section title, so reading only the titles tells the story (the consulting-deck principle Thor wants).
- Minimal text. No paragraphs where a line will do.
- No eyebrow labels over big titles, no numbered 01/02/03 markers on things that are not steps, no emoji, no em dashes in copy.
- Department symbols are flat, colourful and sit straight on the card with no circle or square behind them.
- Motion is slow and small. Cards rise into view; nothing flashes or loops fast. Everything respects reduced motion.
- Touch targets at least 44 pixels. Real buttons and links. Text contrast at least 4.5:1.
